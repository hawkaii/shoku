Delete your account

Last changed 6 November 2026

From the app

Open You, find your account at the top, and tap Delete account. Confirm once. The app erases the copy on your phone and asks the server to erase the rest, then signs you out.

What is deleted

Your dishes, every version of them, your cook logs, your fridge items and shopping list, the photos you added (the rows and the stored images), your assistant conversations and their daily counters, and the account itself including your email address. It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What stays

A reel that was imported keeps its entry on the Wall, because that entry describes the video and its creator, not the person who imported it — the database keeps no link between the two. Nothing else is kept.

If you cannot open the app

Write to hello@gradientdescent.wiki from the email you signed up with and the account is deleted for you within seven days. No reason is needed.
