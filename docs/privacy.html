Privacy

Last changed 6 November 2026

What is stored

Your email address, so you can sign in. Your journal: dishes, recipe versions, cook logs, the photos you add, your fridge items and shopping list, and your conversation with the assistant. All of it belongs to your account, and a copy lives on your phone so the app works offline.

Who processes it

Supabase authenticates you and holds the database. Google Cloud Storage holds the photos you upload, under a key derived from your account. Meta's Model API reads reels and answers your questions; the content sent to it is the reel link and caption, the recipe a question is about, and the question itself. None of these receive your email addresses for advertising, and none of them are allowed to use your content for their own purposes.

What is sent, and what is not kept

A reel link and its caption when you import, so the recipe can be read out of it. A receipt photo when you ask for one to be read: the image is sent once, the lines come back for you to confirm, and the image itself is not stored. Questions you ask the assistant, together with the recipe they are about.

What the Wall shows

The Wall lists imported recipes with their thumbnail and the original creator. It never shows who imported them, and the database keeps no such link. Any card can be reported, and enough reports remove it.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no location. The app carries no advertising or analytics SDK. This page sets no cookies.

Deleting your data

You → Delete account erases your dishes, versions, cooks, fridge, photos, conversations and the account itself, on the server and on the phone, and signs you out. What survives is a reel's Wall entry, because it describes the video and its creator rather than the person who imported it. The full detail is on the deletion page.

Children

Shoku is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly keep their data.
